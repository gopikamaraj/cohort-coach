# Mentor help

## Who a mentor is

A mentor is a teacher whose class fixed the same mistake that a cohort is working on. Each cohort of fifteen to twenty teachers has one mentor. Some mentors are proven: their class's share of children with the mistake fell clearly from one term to the next and by more than the comparison group's average. Others are candidates: their class has very few children with the mistake while the block average is high, and a method interview comes first. A candidate is labelled a candidate until a term confirms it.

## How do I record my method

Recording a method inside the app is not built yet. The plan is simple: you answer a few questions about how you fixed the mistake, by voice or in writing, and the system turns your own sentences into a method card, keeping your exact words. You read it, change it, and approve it. Until that is built, the mentor page shows where each of your items stands, and your coach can tell you what is waiting.

## What happened with my card

Each item you submit moves through these states: draft, submitted, awaiting the coach, then changes requested, approved or rejected. An approved item becomes in use only once a published track version includes it. If you edit an item that is in use, a new draft is made and the old one stays in use until the new one is approved. Nothing you record reaches a teacher before the coach approves it.

## What does the coach check

The coach checks that your card says only what you said, that the steps are safe and plain, that the maths is right, and that nothing identifies a child. A coach can return a card with one reason: wrong maths, culturally off, too complex for the medium, tone issue, or factually shaky. A returned card tells you which.

## What you can see

You see counts for your own method cards, and a count is shown only if it covers at least five classes. You cannot see which teachers tried your card, and you cannot see any teacher's class.

## Your time

A mentor's time is capped at about thirty minutes a week.

## The cohort thread

Your cohort has a small chat thread. Names are pseudonyms, no phone numbers or handles are shown, and a child's name cannot be posted. You can answer a teacher's question there. The coach moderates the thread. The assistant never writes a post in the thread.
