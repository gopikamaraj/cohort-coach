# Judge test script (rebuilt 6 Nov 2026)

This is the written form of the guided tour (`#/tour/1` to `#/tour/9`, about 5 minutes on a laptop). It follows one teacher's path, then the staff pages. The earlier script (v3.1, 21 Oct) described a no-login demo, a bot flow and an "Advance to Term 2" step; none of those exist now.

## Before you start

- **Sign in with a nickname.** There is no password: the nickname is the user id, and what you enter is saved to the cloud under it, readable only by you.
- **Nickname `3A Math` or `3B Math`**, as the role **Teacher**: pilot class data (real counts from pilot classes, not simulated).
- **Nickname `Block Officer`**, as the role **Officer**: the officer page with real counts from the classes entered. Aggregates only; no teacher or child is named.
- **Any other new nickname** starts empty. The demo class and the 24 other classes in the judge pages are simulated and labelled **Simulated**.
- The mentor and coach pages hold **mocked data**, labelled simulated. They show the flow, not real classes.

Open the landing page (`#/roles`), read **How to use**, and press **Guided tour (5 min)**, or open a step by its link.

## The tour

| Step | Link | Role | Time | What to do | What to notice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `#/tour/1` (opens `#/papers`) | Teacher | 30 s | Create a paper: from the rules (Paper Studio), or upload a PDF or an answered sheet | Papers are shared with every teacher; a person confirms what a PDF read found |
| 2 | `#/tour/2` (opens `#/check`) | Teacher | 35 s | Enter a check: choose the paper, type answers or add sheet photos, approve each roll number | The reader never sees the expected answers; you confirm the boxes it was unsure of; if a sheet read empty, use Read this sheet again |
| 3 | `#/tour/3` (opens `#/cards`) | Teacher | 30 s | See the results: counts of children (for example 11 of 28) | Each mistake shows the question, the expected answer, what the child wrote and the diagnosis, as "most likely"; the rule control switches confirmed (two questions) and possible (one) |
| 4 | `#/tour/4` (opens `#/cohort`) | Teacher | 35 s | See your cohort: create one for each mistake, open its improvement track and modules, print the pack | One cohort per mistake; the printed pack carries the answer sheets (kept sheets only) |
| 5 | `#/tour/5` (opens `#/practice`) | Teacher | 45 s | Try the Practice Class with its five simulated children | Private: transcripts are saved to your account and readable only by you |
| 6 | `#/tour/6` (opens `#/profile`) | Teacher | 20 s | See your profile: classes, consent choices (share counts, keep sheets for 60 days), data used | Choices are yours, withdrawable, and recorded |
| 7 | `#/tour/7` (opens `#/officer`) | Officer | 45 s | See the officer page with real counts; Create a cohort by mistake, or Create all cohorts | Aggregates only; cohorts and funnel steps name their source; simulated rows are labelled |
| 8 | `#/tour/8` (opens `#/mentor`) | Mentor | 30 s | See the mentor page (mocked data) | A mentor's recording becomes content only after a coach approves it |
| 9 | `#/tour/9` (opens `#/coach`) | Coach | 30 s | See the coach page (mocked data) | A person approves; nothing is approved by itself |
