# Model lesson template (a script the teacher reads to her class)

A model lesson shows the move done well with a small group of children. The teacher's lines come from the approved move; the child's lines sound like an eight-year-old. Class numbers appear only as slots ({{class.example_a}}, {{class.example_b}}, {{class.example_correct_answer}}). A child's intentional wrong answer carries `[child: 37]`.

## Materials

Bundles of ten sticks, loose sticks, a slate for each child.

## Timing

About twelve minutes: setup (2), the move with three examples (7), the check (2), wrap-up (1).

## Script

Alternate lines, each starting with the speaker:

- Teacher says: a short, clear line.
- Child says: a short child line, in the child's own words.

Use three examples with growing difficulty. After each, the teacher asks the child to explain how she got it.

## The check

Close with the check by adding back: add the answer to the smaller number and see whether it gives the bigger one. The check by adding back is the last thing the children do.
