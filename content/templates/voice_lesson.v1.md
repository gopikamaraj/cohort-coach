Write for a teacher who listens on her own phone. Plain spoken words, short sentences, numbers as digits. About 550 to 650 spoken words. Class numbers appear only as the slots below, never as a real count or a percentage. A child's wrong answer that is meant to be wrong carries the tag `[child: 37]`; every other worked sum must be correct.

Slots you may use: {{class.count_showing}} of {{class.children_checked}} children, {{class.example_a}}, {{class.example_b}}, {{class.example_wrong_answer}}, {{class.example_correct_answer}}, {{track.error_name}}, {{track.day}} and {{track.total_days}}.

## Hook (30 seconds)

Say what today is about in one line, and why it matters to her class: {{class.count_showing}} of {{class.children_checked}} children made this mistake.

## The mistake in one child's words (60 seconds)

Describe the mistake as a child would say it. Use the example: {{class.example_a}} and {{class.example_b}}. The child writes {{class.example_wrong_answer}}. The right answer is {{class.example_correct_answer}}.

## The move (120 seconds)

Teach the one move for today, from the track's strategy list, step by step with bundles of ten sticks. One new idea per lesson. Name the strategy in the words teachers already use.

## A two-line script to use (30 seconds)

Give two short lines she can say in class, one to ask and one to respond.

## Try tomorrow (30 seconds)

One small thing to try with one child, at the practice level the track names (level 1 without regrouping, level 2 with regrouping). Never a number about how many children will improve.

## Reflection prompt (30 seconds)

One question to think about, with an invitation to send a voice note. No score, no judgement.
